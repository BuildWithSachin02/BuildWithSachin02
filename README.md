## 🚀 Featured Projects

### SR Khata — Telegram Business Ledger Bot
`Node.js` · `MongoDB` · `Telegram Bot API`

A two-bot Telegram system (a Customer Bot and an Owner Bot) for managing a shop's customer ledger through a conversational interface. Customers can log purchases, request payment verification, and request undos; owners approve or reject those requests, receive notifications, and pull reports.

- Modular structure (`models`, `services`, separate bot entry points) covering purchases, payment requests, undo requests, and owner reporting
- Service-level tests for balances, duplicate/conflict protection, approval race conditions, and data cleanup
- Currently hardening database consistency under concurrent approval requests, with multi-shop support and staff roles planned next
